A question carries three things that belong to the set it is in rather than to the question: its category (a row in that set's distribution), its tags (rows owned by that set), and its author (someone on that set). Moving used to take the destination and go, which left the category pointing at a distribution the new set does not use and dropped every tag without saying so. This page says what will happen to each and lets it be settled before the move rather than found afterwards. {% endcomment %}
