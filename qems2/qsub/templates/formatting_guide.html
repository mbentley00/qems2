Formatting Guide

Answers should be wrapped with _underscores_.

Prompts should be wrapped with __double underscores__.

Italicize titles by using ~tildes~.

Pronunciation guides use (parentheses), unless the parentheses are escaped with \(backslashes\).

Pronunciation-guide targets (optional): wrap the word or words a guide covers in \P markers, like Denis \PDiderot\P ("DID-er-OW"). They show in teal and don't count toward length. In the editor, select the words and their guide and click PG.

Notes to the moderator or players: wrap them in \N markers, like \NDescription acceptable.\N. Notes print in grey italics and don't count toward length. In the editor, select the sentence and click Note.

Superscripts follow the format \SMy Superscript Text\S.

Subscripts follow the format \sMy subscript text\s.

Powers (15 points) end with (*).

Superpowers (20 points, optional) end with (+), placed before the (*). Text before (+) is worth 20, between (+) and (*) 15.

Bonus part difficulties: mark each part [10e], [10m] or [10h], or end the last answer line with (emh), one letter per part in order. A per-part mark wins.

Read Answer Carefully: check it for complex answer lines. The packet lists these answers at the start.

Pasting plain text: to paste without formatting, use Ctrl+Shift+V (Windows/Linux) or ⌘+Shift+V (Mac).
