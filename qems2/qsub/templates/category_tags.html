Tags are editor-defined requirements — for instance “Asian Literature” under Literature - World with 6 tossups and 6 bonuses needed. Writers fulfill a tag by checking it on the question's edit page. Tags with a required count of 0 are informational labels. A tag can belong to one category or to the whole set. Use a set-wide tag for anything that cuts across the distribution — a time period, a region, where a question came from — instead of creating the same tag once per category: it is offered on every question whatever its category, and its quota counts every question carrying it. A tag can set a ceiling instead of (or as well as) a minimum: “This Empire Indicator” with a maximum of 7 tossups and 0 bonuses asks for none, but goes red once an eighth turns up. Tag names are printed in exported packets unless the tag says otherwise; the document view always shows them.

Tags can also have groups such as location, time period, or other divisions.
