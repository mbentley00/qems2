Tags are editor-defined requirements within a category — for instance “Asian Literature” under Literature - World with 6 tossups and 6 bonuses needed. Writers fulfill a tag by checking it on the question's edit page. Tags with a required count of 0 are informational labels.

A group names the axis a tag runs along — Time, Location, Subject. A category's tags usually run along more than one at once, and naming the axis is what keeps a long list from reading as one flat pile. Tags within a group are alphabetical until you move one with the arrows; from then on the group keeps the order you give it.
